# Privacy Policy - Letterboxd Quick Search

Last updated: November 15, 2026

## What the extension does

Letterboxd Quick Search adds a context-menu option to search for selected text on Letterboxd. When you select text on a webpage and explicitly click "Search on Letterboxd," the extension reads that selected text, constructs a Letterboxd search URL, and opens the URL in a new tab.

## Information handled

- The extension handles only the text you deliberately select for a Letterboxd search.
- The extension does not store, log, or send selected text to the extension developer or any server controlled by the developer.
- The extension does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or an account system.
- The extension does not inspect your browsing history or read the contents of webpages beyond the text passed to it by the browser after your explicit menu action.

## Sharing with Letterboxd

The selected search text is included in the URL of the Letterboxd search-results page. When your browser opens that page, Letterboxd receives the search request and may process associated information in accordance with Letterboxd's own privacy policy. The extension is independent of Letterboxd and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Letterboxd.

## Permissions

The extension requests only the `contextMenus` permission so it can add a search action to the browser's right-click menu.

## Policy changes

If the extension's data-handling behavior changes, this policy should be updated before those changes are published.
